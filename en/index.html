Approach

How does anythink work?

Less noise, visible progress. Communication stays short: it’s always clear what shipped, what’s running, and what’s next.

  1. Figure it out

    Goals, constraints, and success metrics before the first production line.

  3. Keep it running

    Stabilize, document, hand off — or stay on as the product keeps growing.

About

Who is behind anythink?

D

David

Founder & CEO · the mastermind

anythink s.r.o. is founded and led by David. The studio is registered in Šestajovice (Žižkova 411), operates mainly from Prague and nearby, and takes work nationwide across CZ.

He started as a waiter, then bartender, later a manager. When Covid hit, he moved into IT and put nearly everything into programming — learn fast, do better, until the effort actually paid off.

First stop was a company of around twenty people. He moved quickly into a smaller firm focused on its own product, then ran multiple contracts at once while trying his own things. At O2ITS he helped build a product. That path became anythink — same bar: technical, direct, no sugarcoating.

  1. Start

    Hospitality Waiter → bartender → manager. Operations, people, pace.

  2. Covid

    Shift into IT Full focus on programming — fast learning, better craft.

  3. Growth

    ~20 people → product company Then parallel contracts and side experiments.

  4. O2ITS

    Product engineering Building a product in a team, before anythink.

  5. Now

    anythink s.r.o. Founder & CEO — house products and client work.
